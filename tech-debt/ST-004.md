# [ST-004] Point-in-time top-500 universe
[← index](../TECH_DEBT.md)

**Plan doc:** universe = Nifty 500 (live). For backtests, keep delisted and merged stocks and rebuild a point-in-time
"top 500 by traded value" universe as the Nifty 500 stand-in (removes survivorship bias).

**Decided 2026-10-11 (Rakesh):**
- Align with NSE's Nifty 500 review: semi-annual, cut-off last trading day of January and July, six months of data
  ending at the cut-off, trading frequency >= 90% of sessions; membership effective from the first session of April
  and October (after NSE's four-week notice). Source: niftyindices.com Nifty 500 factsheet.
- Ranking proxy: median daily traded value over the six months (NSE ranks by full market cap; no free history of
  shares outstanding). Overlap with the real Nifty 500 is measured first; the threshold is agreed after that.
- Price panel (`data/panel/equity_daily.parquet`) is built in ST-004; data model in `docs/DATA_MODEL.md`.
- Database (ST-012) comes after ST-010, before execution issues.

## Design
1. `ingestion/build_price_panel.py` — every equity row 2014..today (`core.securities.is_equity`) into one Parquet table
   (schema in `docs/DATA_MODEL.md`), raw prices; appends new days on re-run.
2. Series breaks: a new `series_id` when a symbol has no close for > 30 days AND its ISIN changes (INDOSOLAR,
   SPENTEX, NTL, EASTSILK).
3. Universe rule per cut-off, using only data up to the cut-off close: equity, series EQ on the cut-off date, traded
   on >= 90% of sessions in the window, close >= Rs 10, top 500 by median traded value. Written to
   `data/universe/membership.csv`.
4. `core/universe.py` — `members_on(date)` (keys of the membership in force), `rebalance_dates()`.
5. `scripts/validate_universe.py` — panel coverage, no-lookahead recompute, survivorship (known delisted names present
   before their collapse), overlap with today's actual Nifty 500 (NSE constituents file), turnover per review.

**Progress 2026-10-14:** data model approved; panel builder merged (`1615e1e`) and built on prod: 5,399,886 rows,
3,156 trading days 2014-01-01..2026-10-13, 3,127 companies, 3,136 series, 165.4 MB, 59 s. Laptop trial: a full build
and a half-year build + append gave identical 2021 panels (411,041 rows, no nulls). Series breaks: 9 companies, all
post-insolvency relistings with a new ISIN after a long suspension (8 months to 11.5 years; price x0.28 to x165) —
WAAREEINDO (INDOSOLAR), CLCIND (SPENTEX), NEUEON (NTL), EASTSILK, KSE, DSKULKARNI, MBECL, BURNPUR, SUMEETINDS.
Accepted as correct. Next: universe rule + `core/universe.py`.

**Universe built (laptop trial on the prod panel, 2026-10-14):** 25 reviews, cut-offs 2014-07-31..2026-07-31, each
500 members; first universe effective 2014-10-01 (backtests start there). Eligible per review grows ~1,030 (2014) to
~1,830 (2026); turnover 44-88 names per review. Overlap with NSE's current Nifty 500 list (ind_nifty500list.csv,
501 names) vs our review effective 2026-10-01: 414 = **82.6%**; our top 100: 99 in the real index. Misses: large but
thinly traded names (BERGEPAINT, CRISIL, ATUL, AIAENG) and recent IPOs; extras: heavily traded small caps.

**Decided 2026-10-14 (Rakesh):**
- Fidelity alarm: overlap >= **80%** of the real list AND >= **95 of our top 100** in it (today 82.6% / 99).
- New listings: **conservative** — no allowance; a new listing needs >= 90% of the full six-month window, so it joins
  at the first review after ~6 months of trading.
- Survivorship: 5 named spot checks (JETAIRWAYS, DHFL, YESBANK, HDFC, IDEA present in reviews before their collapse /
  merger) **plus** an automatic check: every review with a cut-off before 2024 has >= 1 member that no longer trades.
- Panel coverage and no-lookahead checks as designed.

**Validator laptop trial 2026-10-14 (`--skip-coverage`, prod panel via share):** no lookahead 25/25 identical, none
short; survivorship 5/5 named (JETAIRWAYS 10 reviews, last 2019-04-01; DHFL 8, 2018-04-02; YESBANK 11, 2019-10-01;
HDFC 18, 2023-04-03; IDEA 10, 2019-04-01) and all 19 pre-2024 reviews hold members that no longer trade (98 in 2014
down to 11 in mid-2023); fidelity 82.6% / 99. Coverage runs on prod.

**Done when:** on prod, `python -m scripts.validate_universe` prints RESULT: PASS — panel covers every trading day
2014..today; membership exists for every review; recomputing each review with data cut at its cut-off gives the
identical list; known delisted names are present before their collapse; latest overlap with the real Nifty 500 >= the
agreed threshold (80% overall and 95 of our top 100).
**Fail if:** any member was chosen with data after its cut-off; a review has fewer than 500 members while more than
500 stocks are eligible; or the latest overlap is below the agreed threshold.
