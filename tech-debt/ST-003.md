Out of scope: rights, mergers, demergers.

**Decided 2026-10-11 (Rakesh, option A):** ST-003 covers history only. Merging MyInvestIQ's table (read-only role)
moves to ST-012. Reason: backtests hold out 2024–2026 until a strategy's final check, and MyInvestIQ's table is
itself stale since 2026-08-25 until its daily-fetch TD is done.

**What exists (read 2026-10-11, read-only):** MyInvestIQ `core/corporate_actions.py` (TD-211 B3) — Bonus `X:Y` →
factor `(Y+X)/Y`; Split "From Rs A To Rs B" → `A/B`; traps: NCRPS "Bonus" is not equity, never join on the feed's
`isin` (join by symbol), store only `ex_date < today`. Table `(symbol, ex_date, action_type, adjustment_factor,
subject_text, isin)`; a price dated before ex_date is divided by the cumulative factor.

**Found 2026-10-11 (2021 trial of the validator):** the feed files past actions under the company's CURRENT symbol
while bhavcopy has the symbol in use then — CCAVENUE/INFIBEAM bonus 2021-03-18, VEEDOL/TIDEWATER bonus+split
2021-07-26, DHARAN/KARDA bonus+split 2021-08-12 (4 of 57 actions in 2021). Legacy PREVCLOSE is raw (54/54), so the
PREVCLOSE cross-check is not possible. DVL "Bonus 1:2" 2021-08-05 never moved the price (same ISIN) — feed error.
**Decided (Rakesh, 2026-10-11, option a):** ST-003 also builds the rename list (NSE `symbolchange.csv`,
`core/symbol_changes.py`); each action is stored under the symbol in use on its ex_date. Moved here from ST-013,
which keeps price-series stitching.

## Design
1. `ingestion/fetch_corporate_actions_history.py` — NSE `corporates-corporateActions` API, one call per calendar
   year 2014..2025 (2025 ends 2025-08-25). Raw JSON kept as `data/corporate_actions/raw/<YYYY>.json`; parsed to
   `data/corporate_actions/history.csv` with MyInvestIQ's columns. Parser **copied, not imported** (as ST-002).
2. Every subject matching `bonus|split|sub-division` is either parsed or excluded with a named reason (NCRPS, …).
   Per-year counts and any unclassified subjects go to the log.
3. `core/corporate_actions.py` — loads history.csv; `cumulative_factor(symbol, from_date, to_date)` (actions with
   from_date < ex_date <= to_date, multiplied); `adjust_price()` divides price, `adjust_volume()` multiplies.
   Join by symbol as of ex_date (renames are ST-013). No adjusted price panel here (ST-004/010).
4. `scripts/validate_corporate_actions.py` — streams the bhavcopy archive day by day:
   - Jump scan: adjusted close-to-close move on every stored ex_date; every raw one-day move worse than −35% is
     matched to a stored action or listed in `config/ca_exceptions.csv` with a reason.
   - Legacy PREVCLOSE probe: if the legacy file's PREVCLOSE on an ex_date is NSE-adjusted, raw prior close ÷
     PREVCLOSE is an independent factor to compare. If the probe shows it is raw, drop the check and record that.
   - Spot check: 5 known events against yfinance adjusted closes.
5. Tests on the laptop: parser and adjuster against MyInvestIQ's fixture JSON (copied to `tests/fixtures/`), plus a
   one-year live fetch. Full fetch and validation on prod.

**Added 2026-10-11 (Rakesh approved items 2 and 3):** jump scan covers equities only (ISIN INE/IN9, no `-RE<n>`),
skipped symbols counted by ISIN prefix; drops from a close below Rs 1 are tick moves. `config/ca_overrides.csv`
(VOID/ADD, applied by `load_history`; a stale VOID raises). `config/symbol_changes_extra.csv` adds renames missing
from NSE's list (PHILIPCARB->PCBL, INTEGRA->ESSENTIA). `config/ca_exceptions.csv` explains genuine crashes and
suspension gaps (`drop`), and records capital-reduction decisions (`review`). Each drop row shows whether the ISIN
changed. A stored action never observed in prices now fails (missed rename) unless explained.

**Laptop trial 2026-10-11 (prod data via shares):** 763/763 actions observed, 0 outside +/-30%; 55 renamed;
spot 5/5; 414 raw drops = 78 explained by the feed + 324 tick moves + 12 exceptions; 5 reviews decided; PASS.

**Done when:** on prod, `python -m scripts.validate_corporate_actions` prints RESULT: PASS, with every year
2014..2025 fetched (>= 1 record each), 0 unclassified bonus/split-shaped subjects, every stored action's adjusted
ex-date move within ±30%, and every raw one-day move worse than −35% either matched to a stored action or listed in
`config/ca_exceptions.csv` with a reason.
**Fail if:** any stored split/bonus still shows a move beyond ±30% after adjustment (wrong or unapplied factor), any
stored action is never observed in the price data without a recorded reason (missed rename), or any year returns
0 records.
