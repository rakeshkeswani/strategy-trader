# [ST-013] Symbol changes: stitch price series across renames
[← index](../TECH_DEBT.md)

A rename changes the NSE symbol but usually keeps the ISIN; a split changes the ISIN but keeps the symbol.
Mergers (symbol and ISIN both vanish) stay delistings (survivorship is ST-004).

**Scope moved to ST-003 (Rakesh, 2026-10-11):** the rename list itself — NSE `symbolchange.csv` snapshot
(`ingestion/fetch_symbol_changes.py`), `config/symbol_changes_extra.csv`, and `core/symbol_changes.py`
(`symbol_on`: which symbol a company traded under on a date). **ST-013 keeps:** one continuous identity per company
across renames, proved against prices.

**Problem:** TIDEWATER trades until 2024-10-08 and VEEDOL from 2024-10-09. To a backtest or the point-in-time
universe that is a delisting plus a new listing: momentum lookbacks break and false entries/exits appear.

## Design (approved 2026-10-11; key = current symbol, option a)
1. `core/securities.py`
   - `key_on(changes, symbol, date)` — the company key = the symbol it trades under today (VEEDOL for the whole
     lineage). Follows renames forward from that date; a reused symbol is a different company after its reuse date.
   - `symbols_for(changes, key)` — `[(symbol, from, to)]`, oldest first.
   - `read_stitched_ohlcv(changes, key, start, end)` — one daily series across renames (raw prices, symbol per row).
   - `is_equity(symbol, isin)` moves here (ISIN INE/IN9, not `-RE<n>`) and is shared with the ST-003 validator.
   Key changes if a company renames again; research outputs are regenerated, so acceptable (Rakesh, option a).
2. Renames missing from NSE's list are found by ISIN continuity: symbol A's run ends and symbol B's run starts
   within 5 sessions with the same ISIN. Each candidate is either a known rename (list/extras) or listed in
   `config/symbol_exceptions.csv` with a reason. True misses go to `config/symbol_changes_extra.csv` with evidence.
3. `scripts/validate_symbol_changes.py` streams every trading day 2014..today through `core.prices` (equities only)
   and records each symbol's runs (first/last date, close, ISIN):
   - each listed rename in range is confirmed: the new symbol's first close follows the old symbol's last close
     within 30 calendar days; ISIN unchanged, or the change explained by a stored split/bonus at the stitch; the
     adjusted move across the stitch within ±30%;
   - every continuity candidate is resolved;
   - list entries never seen in equity bhavcopy (MF schemes, pre-2014, SME) are counted, not failed.
4. Tests on real cases: TIDEWATER->VEEDOL, KARDA->KBCGLOBAL->DHARAN, PHILIPCARB->PCBL, a reused symbol, and a fake
   rename with different ISINs that must not confirm.

Out of scope: mergers/delistings (ST-004), the adjusted price panel (ST-004/ST-010).

**Laptop trial 2026-10-11 (prod data via shares, 2014..2026-10-09):** 3,154 trading days, 3,410 equity symbols.
First run: 276 renames confirmed, 6 unresolved continuity candidates — all real renames missing from NSE's list
(RUCHI->PATANJALI, WABCOINDIA->ZFCVINDIA, BURGERKING->RBA, PDSMFL->PDSL, SHRIRAMEPC->SEPC, DALALSTCOM->KEEPLEARN),
added to `config/symbol_changes_extra.csv`. Re-run: 687 renames in range, 282 confirmed, 0 outside +/-30%, 0 unexplained
ISIN changes; 383 never traded as equity, 19 one-sided, 3 long gaps (post-insolvency relistings, info only);
278/278 candidates are known renames; PASS. ST-003 re-checked after the extras and the `is_equity` move: still PASS
(history unchanged, 55 renamed).
**For ST-004:** INDOSOLAR->WAAREEINDO, SPENTEX->CLCIND, NTL->NEUEON relisted after insolvency with a new ISIN — a run
gap > 30 days with an ISIN change must start a new price series.

**Done when:** on prod, `python -m scripts.validate_symbol_changes` prints RESULT: PASS — every listed rename in range
confirmed by continuity, every continuity candidate resolved (extras or exception with reason), every stitch within
±30% after adjustment.
**Fail if:** any confirmed rename's stitch moves beyond ±30% after adjustment, a rename's ISIN changes at the stitch
with no stored split/bonus to explain it (and no exception), or any continuity candidate (same ISIN, A ends, B
starts) is left unresolved.
