# [ST-019] Demergers (spin-offs): price adjustment
[← index](../TECH_DEBT.md)

**Raised 2026-10-15 (ST-010 re-run).** rakesh_top4 showed single lots at -91% / -89%. All were MIRZAINT around
29 Mar 2023: close 252.85 -> 34.65 (-86%) in one day. Cause: the RedTape demerger, record and ex-date 29 Mar 2023,
1 RedTape share per Mirza share; RedTape listed 11 Aug 2023 (BSE filing 31 Mar 2023: the price move was "due to Spin
Off Corporate Action"). ST-003 covered splits and bonuses only ("rights, mergers, demergers ... out of scope"), so the
backtest sees a crash; the -15% rule then sold the lots at -83% to -91% and the RedTape shares the holder received
are missing. Any held stock that demerged has the same false loss.

**Decided (Rakesh, 2026-10-15, option a):** treat a demerger like a split: a factor on the parent's earlier prices
equal to the ex-day price ratio, so a lot keeps its value across the ex-date (as if the holder kept the spun-off
shares inside the parent). An approximation (the new shares really trade separately, often months later) and the
usual total-return practice. Not chosen: (b) model the new company's shares; (c) leave unadjusted and only flag.

**Inputs:** ST-010 validator check 9 (held stock moved more than +/-30% in a day, not a known crash) lists the
demergers that hit the three runs. To scope with the design: where the demerger list comes from (NSE corporate-action
feed "demerger" records already read by ST-003, plus MyInvestIQ's table after TD-251 / ST-012), how the factor is set
(previous close / ex-day reference price, or the official cost-of-acquisition split where published), and the same
for the live system (ST-008/009: a demerger day must not fire the -15% rule or GTT stops).

**Scope found 2026-10-15:** the stored NSE feed (`raw/*.json`, 2014..2025-08) has 135 EQ demerger / scheme-of-arrangement
records; 69 are stocks that were ever in the universe and gapped > 5% on the ex-date (e.g. TATACHEM -57% 2020, SUVEN
-95% 2020, IDFC -51% 2015, GRASIM -18% 2017, RELIANCE -9% 2023, SIEMENS -50% 2025). Beyond held lots, each one also
distorts ret_252 / ret_126 / sma_200 for a year, i.e. the rankings both strategies use.

**Design (Rakesh, 2026-10-15):**
1. Source: demerger / scheme records already in the stored feed (EQ series); after 2025-08-25 via ST-012.
2. Factor = close before the ex-date / **close** on the ex-date (price-based; the official cost-split ratio was not
   chosen). Changed from the open (Rakesh, 2026-10-15): the open is often not the discovered price -- ADANIENT
   2015-06-03 opened 573.30 (prev 637.00) and closed 109.75; SINTEX, IDFC, NMDC, SIEMENS likewise.
3. Applied only when the price FELL by more than 5% (value leaving the company can only lower the price); smaller falls
   and any rise are listed as not applied (INTELLECT 2015 +5.8% NCD option, ARVIND 2015 +6.3%, UEL 2024 +267%).
4. `config/demergers.csv`, generated by a script, every row with subject, prices, factor, applied, note; Claude
   spot-checks big and borderline rows against news; Rakesh approves the list.
5. `core/adjusted.py` applies these factors exactly like splits (indicators, lots, validator all see them).
6. Re-run the three ST-010 backtests, validator and cards; show before/after.
7. Note to ST-008/009: on a live demerger day the -15% rule and GTT stops must not fire on the gap.

**Done when:** every applied row (MIRZAINT 2023-03-29 included) has no adjusted one-day move beyond +/-10% on the
ex-date or the 2 trading days after it, unless its note explains it (changed from "ex-date move within +/-5%", which a
close-based factor meets by construction; Rakesh 2026-10-15); validator
check 9 shows no unexplained move in any of the three ST-010 runs; all three re-runs PASS the validator on prod; Rakesh
has approved `config/demergers.csv`.
**Laptop result 2026-10-15:** `config/demergers.csv` = 135 feed records: 94 applied, 41 not (22 no EQ trade around
the ex-date, 16 falls within 5%, 3 rises). `build_demergers --check` PASS; 4 rows REVIEWED (MIRZAINT and JINDALSAW
source-checked; SINTEX and RADIOCITY moves after the ex-date explained). 74 demerger factors fall inside the ST-010 run
window. Re-runs with `--rerun` validation: all three PASS, check 9 = 0 unexplained moves, no warnings.

**Fail if:** an applied row has an unexplained adjusted move beyond +/-10% in those 3 days, a demerger in a held lot still shows as a crash, or a
record with a gap beyond 5% is neither applied nor explained in its note.
