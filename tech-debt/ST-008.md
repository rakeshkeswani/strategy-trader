# [ST-008] Guardrail service + guardrails.yaml
[← index](../TECH_DEBT.md)

Scope to be proposed before work starts. See the plan doc.

**Decided inputs (Rakesh):**
- Limits so far: Rs 10,000 new buys/week; Rs 1,00,000 total at cost of open stratrad lots; Rs 40,000 per strategy;
  Rs 25,000 per sector; ~Rs 1,000 risk per trade (stop ~8-10% away); rolling 12-month loss tiers 10% alert / 15% no new
  buys / 20% halt.
- **Per-stock cap (2026-10-14): 8% of the overall capital cap** (Rs 8,000 at Rs 1,00,000), across all stratrad
  strategies. A setting in `guardrails.yaml`, not a hard number — may be raised if it proves too tight. Consequence at
  today's numbers: one lot per stock of <= Rs 8,000, risking ~Rs 640-800 at an 8-10% stop; >= 13 stocks at full cap.
- Caps count stratrad lots only; Rakesh's own holdings (strategy `manual`, docs/DATA_MODEL.md) never count.
- Guardrails run twice: before proposing and just before ordering (docs/DATA_MODEL.md section 5).
- From ST-003: genuine news-driven crashes happen (YESBANK -56%, INFIBEAM -71% in a day) — handle gap-downs through a
  stop, and consider news checks before buys.
- From ST-019 (2026-10-15): on a demerger ex-date the parent's price drops by the value spun off (MIRZAINT -86%,
  TATACHEM -57%), which is not a loss -- the holder receives the new shares. Guardrails and exits (the -15% rule, loss
  tiers, GTT stops via ST-009) must know upcoming demerger ex-dates and not fire on that gap; lot cost must be split
  between parent and new company (the factor from `config/demergers.csv`, or the official cost split).

**Done when:** to be written when the design is agreed (observable behaviour + a falsifying clause).
