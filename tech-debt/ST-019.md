# [ST-019] Demergers (spin-offs): price adjustment
[← index](../TECH_DEBT.md)

**Raised 2026-10-15 (ST-010 re-run).** rakesh_top4 showed single lots at -91% / -89%. All were MIRZAINT around
29 Mar 2023: close 252.85 -> 34.65 (-86%) in one day. Cause: the RedTape demerger, record and ex-date 29 Mar 2023,
1 RedTape share per Mirza share; RedTape listed 11 Aug 2023 (BSE filing 31 Mar 2023: the price move was "due to Spin
Off Corporate Action"). ST-003 covered splits and bonuses only ("rights, mergers, demergers ... out of scope"), so the
backtest sees a crash; the -15% rule then sold the lots at -83% to -91% and the RedTape shares the holder received
are missing. Any held stock that demerged has the same false loss.

**Decided (Rakesh, 2026-10-15, option a):** treat a demerger like a split: a factor on the parent's earlier prices
equal to the ex-day price ratio, so a lot keeps its value across the ex-date (as if the holder kept the spun-off
shares inside the parent). An approximation (the new shares really trade separately, often months later) and the
usual total-return practice. Not chosen: (b) model the new company's shares; (c) leave unadjusted and only flag.

**Inputs:** ST-010 validator check 9 (held stock moved more than +/-30% in a day, not a known crash) lists the
demergers that hit the three runs. To scope with the design: where the demerger list comes from (NSE corporate-action
feed "demerger" records already read by ST-003, plus MyInvestIQ's table after TD-251 / ST-012), how the factor is set
(previous close / ex-day reference price, or the official cost-of-acquisition split where published), and the same
for the live system (ST-008/009: a demerger day must not fire the -15% rule or GTT stops).

**Done when:** to be written with the design. At least: MIRZAINT 2023-03-29 adjusted; validator check 9 on the three
ST-010 runs lists no unexplained move; rakesh_top4 re-run without -80% lots from demergers.
