# [ST-010] Baseline momentum strategy + quick backtest
[← index](../TECH_DEBT.md)

Scope to be proposed before work starts. See the plan doc.

**Decided inputs for the design (Rakesh, 2026-10-14):**
- The backtest simulates **lots**: each weekly buy is a lot with its own entry, stop and exit; a stock may be held by
  several strategies; results per strategy from its own lots, portfolio results across all stratrad lots.
- Apply all limits from the start, incl. the **per-stock cap = 8% of the capital cap** (one lot per stock, ~Rs 8,000).
- Universe: ST-004's point-in-time Nifty 500 stand-in (backtests start 2014-10-01).
- Tax: portfolio-level STCG (holds 1-6 months); per-lot tax not modelled.

**Open for the design proposal:** exact baseline rule; weekly calendar and fill assumptions; costs (confirm current
STT, stamp duty, exchange/SEBI charges, GST; STCG rate); Nifty 500 index history 2014-2023 (MyInvestIQ's index archive
starts 2024); sector cap before ST-005 exists; adjusted price series at read time (ST-003 factors).

**Done when:** to be written when the design is agreed (observable behaviour + a falsifying clause).
