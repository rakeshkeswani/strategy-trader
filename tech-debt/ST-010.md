# [ST-010] Baseline momentum strategy + quick backtest
[← index](../TECH_DEBT.md)

Scope to be proposed before work starts. See the plan doc.

**Decided inputs for the design (Rakesh, 2026-10-14):**
- The backtest simulates **lots**: each weekly buy is a lot with its own entry, stop and exit; a stock may be held by
  several strategies; results per strategy from its own lots, portfolio results across all stratrad lots.
- Apply all limits from the start, incl. the **per-stock cap = 8% of the capital cap** (one lot per stock, ~Rs 8,000).
- Universe: ST-004's point-in-time Nifty 500 stand-in (backtests start 2014-10-01).
- Tax: portfolio level (superseded in detail by Design decision 5 below: STCG and LTCG by holding period).

## Design (approved by Rakesh 2026-10-14)

**Verified 2026-10-14:**
- Zerodha equity delivery (zerodha.com/charges): brokerage 0; STT 0.1% buy and sell; NSE transaction 0.00307%; SEBI
  Rs 10/crore; stamp 0.015% buy side; GST 18% on (brokerage + SEBI + transaction); DP Rs 15.34 per stock per sell day.
  A Rs 8,000 round trip costs ~Rs 33 (~0.4%).
- Tax: STCG 20% (s.111A) + 4% cess = 20.8%; LTCG 12.5% + cess = 13% for listed equity held more than 12 months.
- Benchmark: Nifty 500 price index from Yahoo `^CRSLDX`, 2013-12-02..2026-10-13 (3,162 days); cross-checked against
  MyInvestIQ's daily index files (2024+). Price index vs a strategy without dividends: like for like.

**Decisions (Rakesh):**
1. Baseline rule (plan doc): top by 6-month return, close above the 200-day average, RSI(14) 50-70; universe = ST-004
   membership in force. Variants (e.g. 12-1 momentum) go to the research library, not the baseline.
2. Stops/exits: initial stop 8% below entry; trailing 8% below the highest close since entry; **time exit on the first
   trading day after the lot has been held more than 12 months** (purchase date + 1 year + 1 day), so a lot still above
   its trailing stop becomes long-term (LTCG) — "gain the rise and the CG saving". Most lots still exit earlier on the
   trailing stop.
3. The baseline may use the full Rs 1,00,000 until a second strategy goes live; the Rs 40,000 per-strategy cap applies
   from then.
4. No sector cap until ST-005; report sector concentration afterwards with NSE's current industry labels (approximate).
5. Tax: today's rates for all years (STCG 20.8%, LTCG 13%), at portfolio level per financial year, losses set off
   within the year; the LTCG exemption is not modelled (Rakesh's gains exceed it). FIFO in the shared account may shift
   real tax slightly (docs/DATA_MODEL.md).
6. Develop and inspect on 2014-10-01..2023-12-31 only; run 2024-01-01..today once at the end, reported separately, no
   changes after.

**Mechanics:** signals at Friday close on split/bonus-adjusted prices (ST-003 factors at read time); buy at Monday
open + 0.1% slippage; lot = min(Rs 8,000 per-stock cap, Rs 1,000 / stop distance, weekly budget left); stop hit
intraday -> sell at the stop; open below the stop (gap-down) -> sell at the open; all limits applied daily (Rs 1,00,000
at cost, Rs 8,000 per stock, Rs 10,000 new buys/week, loss tiers 10/15/20%). Rule in
`config/strategies/baseline_momentum.yaml`. Output per run in `data/backtests/<run_id>/`: config, lots (entry, stop,
exit, reason, charges), daily portfolio value, metrics (CAGR, max drawdown, worst rolling 12-month loss, hit rate,
average hold, costs, tax, vs Nifty 500).

**Strategy verdict (Rakesh's call, separate from Done-when):** Test 1 loss first — worst rolling 12-month loss within
20% of capital, else rejected whatever the return (plan doc); Test 2 — return after costs and tax vs Nifty 500
buy-and-hold, on 2014-2023 and on the untouched 2024-2026. Calls: go live / go live as plumbing baseline while research
continues / smaller size / adjust and re-test (each re-test recorded, to avoid overfitting).

**Report card (Rakesh, 2026-10-14: wants help reading each strategy's card):** plain-language summary first (e.g. "beat
the Nifty 500 in 7 of 10 years by 3.1%/yr; likely range of the true lead -1.5% to +7.5%"); consistency (share of rolling
12-month periods ahead of the index; best/worst years); a bootstrap confidence range for the lead; risk side by side with
the Nifty 500 (worst 12-month loss, deepest drawdown); charges and tax per year; the 2024-26 run shown separately.
Claude walks Rakesh through each card.

**Dependency for the 2024-26 held-out run:** ST-003's split/bonus history ends 2025-08-25; later actions come from
MyInvestIQ's table (TD-251 + ST-012 merge, not done). Until then a split after Aug 2025 would look like a crash, so the
held-out run waits for ST-012. Development on 2014-2023 is unaffected.

## Build progress
**Piece 1 — adjusted prices (`core/adjusted.py`), 2026-10-14:** each action attaches to the series trading its
symbol on the ex-date (first row on/after it within 45 days). On the prod panel: 762/763 actions matched; on every
ex-date in the panel (754) the adjusted close-to-close move is within +/-30%. Unmatched: RAIREKMOH bonus 2014-02-06 (no
trade within 45 days; illiquid; before the first universe, Oct 2014) — accepted, reported on every run. 10 days
missed VERTOZ's 2025 consolidation, hence 45.

**Done when:** on prod the backtest runs end to end and the same config gives identical results; a validator
confirms no lookahead (changing any future price leaves past decisions unchanged), every limit respected every day,
every lot has an entry, a stop and an exit or is open, charges recompute exactly, and the benchmark is aligned day by
day.
**Fail if:** any limit is breached on any day, a past decision changes when future data changes, or two runs of the
same config differ.
